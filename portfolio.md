## Selected Projects & Research

### Energy-Efficient Enterprise Wireless Networks
**Undergraduate thesis** · University of Chittagong · September 2024–September 2025

**Title:** *Enhancing Energy Efficiency in Enterprise Wireless Networks: A Calendar-Based Power Scheduling Approach for Cisco Catalyst 9800 WLC*

**Supervisor:** Prof. Dr. Atiqur Rahman

Investigated a calendar-based scheduling approach for wireless access points managed through a Cisco Catalyst 9800 Wireless LAN Controller (WLC), with the aim of reducing energy use during lower-demand periods.

**Reported study results**
- Evaluated a scenario involving **100 access points**.
- Used power assumptions of **17.7 W in active mode** and **14 W in sleep mode**.
- Estimated energy savings of **2,334.7 kWh** and a **15.05% reduction** under the study assumptions.
- Estimated electricity-cost savings of **BDT 11,672.50**, using **BDT 5/kWh**.
- Estimated emissions reduction of **1,384.47 kg CO₂**, using an assumed factor of **0.593 kg CO₂/kWh**.

**Approach:** calendar-based power scheduling, scenario analysis, energy-efficiency evaluation, cost estimation, and carbon-impact estimation.

*These figures are estimates based on the study's stated assumptions and should be presented with the methodology when used in a formal publication.*
